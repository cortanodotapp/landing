Overview
--------

This Cookie Notice explains how Cortano Limited ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our websites and apps that link to this notice. It should be read together with our Privacy Notice, which explains how we collect, use, and share personal information.

What are cookies?
-----------------

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store data that can be read by a web server in the domain that placed the cookie. Other technologies, including web beacons, SDKs, pixels, local storage, and similar files, are used for similar purposes. We refer to all of these as "cookies" in this notice.

How we use cookies
------------------

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Keep our site secure and functioning (strictly necessary)
- Remember your preferences (functional)
- Measure and improve performance and user experience (analytics)
- Deliver and measure marketing (only where applicable and permitted)

Types of cookies we use
-----------------------

- Strictly necessary cookies: Required for core functionality such as page navigation, security, and load balancing. These cannot be switched off in our systems.
- Functional cookies: Enable enhanced functionality and personalization, like remembering choices you make.
- Performance/analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our Services so we can measure and improve them.
- Advertising cookies (if used): May be set by us or our partners to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant content or ads. See the Privacy Notice for details and opt-out options.

Analytics and error tracking
----------------------------

We use PostHog to help us understand usage and improve reliability and performance. Depending on your settings, PostHog may set cookies and collect usage data such as pages viewed, feature interactions, device/browser information, and basic event telemetry. We also configure session recording for quality and debugging purposes. For more information about PostHog, visit https://posthog.com and our Privacy Notice.

Consent and how to control cookies
----------------------------------

- Consent: Non-essential cookies (e.g., analytics/marketing) are used only after you provide consent. You can change your mind at any time.
- Preference storage: We store your cookie preference in your browser using local storage under the key "cortano-cookie-consent" (values: "accepted" or "declined"). Clearing site data or using private browsing will remove this preference.
- Browser controls: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Doing so may affect site functionality. Refer to your browser’s help for instructions.
- Interest-based advertising: Where applicable, you can opt out of interest-based advertising on participating sites at http://www.aboutads.info/choices/ (US) or http://www.youronlinechoices.eu/ (EU).

Do Not Track
------------

Some browsers offer a Do Not Track (DNT) signal. There is no current industry standard for responding to DNT signals; as noted in our Privacy Notice, we do not respond to DNT at this time.
